# 💖 For Shaista (Saizzu) — A Bespoke Love Story

A deeply personal, romantic, interactive web application built with love by **Mudassir** for **Shaista**.

---

## ✨ Features & Interactive Elements

- 🌟 **The Hero Entrance**: Ambient particle systems with floating hearts, starlight, and luxury typography.
- 💌 **Wax-Sealed Love Envelope**: Tactile 3D folding envelope that breaks open on tap to reveal a heartfelt Urdu love letter in authentic Nastaliq calligraphy.
- 🎨 **Canvas Scratch-to-Reveal Card**: Metallic golden foil overlay that scratches away to reveal a cherished memory photo.
- ⏳ **Live Relationship Timer**: Real-time ticker counting Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds since 26 November 2023, along with total days together and milestone countdowns.
- 📊 **Our Story in Numbers (Chat Wrapped)**: Spotify-Wrapped style bento grid highlighting 100,000+ texts, 50/50 balance, call hours, top emojis, and the famous *"Sona mat, baat karni hai!"* mystery.
- 🌸 **Spontaneous Flower Burst**: Interactive button that triggers multi-stage screenwide rose blossom explosions.
- 💖 **Reasons I Love You**: 5 index cards pinned with washi tape detailing personal truths.
- 🎡 **Spin the Wheel (Date & Gift Roulette)**: High-resolution physics-based roulette with 7 custom rewards and a real-time prize catalog.
- 🔬 **Love Compatibility Meter**: Algorithmic resonance scanner that always results in **101% Eternal Soulmates**.
- 🎵 **Our Soundtrack (Retro Turntable)**: Spinning vinyl record player with personalized song dedications (*Haan Tu Hai*, *Humdard*, *Banjaara*, *Tujhe Sochta Hoon*).
- 📷 **Polaroid Carousel**: Handwritten polaroid photo frames tracing chapters of shared memories.
- 🌙 **The Moon That Night**: 3D celestial illumination of the exact moon phase on 26 November 2023.
- ✍️ **Closing Note**: Heartfelt closing sign-off from Mudassir.
